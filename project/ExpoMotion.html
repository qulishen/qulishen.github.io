Benchmark and method数据基准与方法

ExpoMotion benchmark

The dataset contains 1,738 sequences and 10,909 images spanning controlled and real-world motion, lab setups, and extreme lighting.数据集包含 1,738 组序列和 10,909 张图像，覆盖受控运动、真实运动、实验室场景和极端照明。

Exposure pre-alignment

Global Priors Illumination Alignment harmonizes exposure levels before deghosting.全局先验光照对齐模块先统一不同帧的曝光。

Householder Orthogonal Attention

A learned Householder reflector projects out ghosting components while retaining scene details.学习得到的 Householder 反射算子投影消除重影成分，同时保留场景细节。
